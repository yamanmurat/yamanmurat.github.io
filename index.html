01 / About

About

I am a computational biochemist (Ph.D., The Graduate Center, CUNY) applying quantum mechanics, molecular dynamics and conformational free energy methods to the structure, dynamics and flexibility of biomolecules, with a track record of validating physics-based models against quantum-mechanical and experimental data from NMR and gas-phase spectroscopy.

My doctoral work, carried out in the Marianski group at Hunter College, asked how external factors shape the conformational free energy landscapes of carbohydrates, from small oligosaccharides to blood group antigens. Alongside the simulations I build production-quality Python software, cheminformatics workflows and HPC pipelines that keep this kind of modeling reproducible.

My background spans computational, physical, analytical and biochemistry, and my research sits close to therapeutics. I am currently looking to apply physics-based molecular modeling to structure-based drug discovery.

Areas I work in

  • Conformational free energy landscapes of carbohydrates and blood group antigens
  • Benchmarking density functionals, basis sets and force fields against QM and experimental observables
  • Mechanistic studies of glycan rearrangements during mass-spectrometric analysis, including ab initio molecular dynamics
  • Open, maintainable scientific software for simulation-data curation, benchmarking and visualization

02 / Research

Research Focus

#1 Quantum Chemistry

Method Benchmarking

Benchmarking density functionals and basis sets for harmonic and anharmonic molecular vibrations, and validating computed spectra against experimental gas-phase measurements.

#2 Dynamics

Free Energy Landscapes

Mapping the conformational free energy landscapes of carbohydrates with classical and ab initio molecular dynamics, DFT conformer free energies and Boltzmann-weighted ensembles.

#3 Infrastructure

Software and HPC

Building Python packages and Slurm-based pipelines that automate curation, benchmarking and publication-quality visualization of large-scale simulation data.

04 / Software

Software

Main developer and maintainer

CarPpy

End-to-end Python pipeline for automated curation and benchmarking of molecular conformers from Gaussian log files: generates and compares IR spectra, computes RMSD and Pendry R-factors, visualizes energetic landscapes, and optimizes structures via a built-in genetic algorithm.

github.com/marianski-lab/CarPpy

Main developer and maintainer

matmalib

Scientific Python library for publication-quality figures and visualization of simulation data from Gaussian 16, GROMACS, ORCA and CP2K: free energy maps, MD trajectories, scatter plots and histograms.

Private repository during an ongoing commercialization and IP process.
